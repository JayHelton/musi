Musi Chord Cards

Printable reference cards for closed, movable guitar shapes — labeled by root and interval (R, 3, b7, 9…), not by key. Standard and drop tuning.

How to read: frets are relative to the root fret (R). × = muted. No open-string shapes — move the whole grip anywhere (usually fret 1+). Drop tuning uses a DADGBE geometry model that transfers to other drop-6 tunings. Prebuilt printable files live in dist/.
